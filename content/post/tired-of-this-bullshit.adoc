some strong feels as I try to navigate this.

<!--more-->

1. i f**king love LLMs for generating code, reviewing blog posts, and doing a million other things that make me more productive.
2. i f**king detest reading LLM-generated material from colleagues.

i am so tired of the imbalance that it creates. it's a variation on https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandolini%27s_law[Brandolini's law]: the energy needed to even read this bullshit is an order of magnitude greater than that taken to prompt a clanker to generate it.

is the answer to play them at their own game, and have an LLM parse and respond to the content, in a race to the bottom of a greasy barrel of sloppy AI banality and emptiness?

i hope not.
