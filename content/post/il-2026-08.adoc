== RDBMS

* 🔥 Alex Chan (Tailscale) - https://tailscale.com/blog/sqlite-wal-reset-bug[How we tracked down a 16-year-old SQLite bug].
* It used to be simple.
A database over here for your transactional workload, one over there for all your heavy analytics, and perhaps a few others scattered around for the cool kids playing with NoSQL or graphs etc.
Over the past few years several databases have started to stretch their support to encompass other workloads.
Rick Houlihan from Oracle considers a possible definition for https://blogs.oracle.com/developers/what-is-a-converged-database-definition-five-tests-and-ai-use-cases[a converged database].
