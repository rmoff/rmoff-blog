It's been a few years since Current (and before that, Kafka Summit) was in San Francisco, but here we are—on November 4 & 5 we'll be there, and I'm excited to continue a tradition that started back in 2019 with a run https://twitter.com/rmoff/status/1179047181891883008[over the Golden Gate Bridge].

This time round we also have some fancy swag from the kind folk at Lenses.
If you want swag, you need to register for the run https://luma.com/lenses-pct9[here].
If you don't want swag and just want to turn up, that's cool too.

== Who? What? Where? When?

* _Who_: Anyone who would like to is very welcome :)
* _What_: A 6.1k (3.8 miles) run or walk
* _Where_: Meet at the https://maps.app.goo.gl/damQiq8kN9wFTjnt8[Golden Gate Bridge Welcome Center] in the car park
* _When_: **Wednesday, 4 November, 7:00 am**.
* _Route_: https://www.strava.com/routes/21896823[It'll be hard to get lost] ;)
