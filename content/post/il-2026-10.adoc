As 2026 hurtles towards the holiday season and then on into 2027, I took a moment to canvass opinion from some of y'all as to the shape of this newsletter.
<!--more-->
For the last year I've included a section on AI, which has grown from a passing note through to a significant proportion of the newsletter (a third of it at times).
So yesterday I asked on both https://interestinglinks.substack.com/p/poll-should-i-create-separate-posts[Substack] and https://lnkd.in/p/d5tBVUMD[LinkedIn] whether I should split this out into its own post.
I figured that some people might want _only_ AI…and some people _no_ AI.

The vote was relatively evenly split, but came down in favour of keeping it as is.
So, that is what I shall do :)
