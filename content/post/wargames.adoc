Having clankers churn out content to feed the clankers is a futile game to play.
It's an arms-race to the bottom of a cesspit.
<!--more-->
Somewhere out there are real humans wanting to read real content, but being deafened by the roar of shit that is destroying the internet as a place to learn and interact with other humans.

image::/images/2026/07/wargames02.gif[The WOPR computer terminal from WarGames flickering as it runs through simulations showing the world being destroyed]

So fuck that, I'm out.
(Technically, link:/ai[I was never in]; writing posts with AI is https://writethatblog.substack.com/p/dev-reaction-to-ai-blog-posts[gross]).

And if folk manage to find it and enjoy reading it, that's wonderful.
And if it gets drowned out in an ocean of slop and never gets found, then I guess I'm just writing for myself.
Which is why I started out writing anyway :)
